your rights

A report is a right, not a favour.

This system enforces what the law already guarantees. Refusing to open a case, losing a docket or altering a record each leave a mark in the audit trail.

National Instruction 3 of 2011
Officers are strictly prohibited from refusing to open a docket based on jurisdiction or perceived triviality.
Section 33 of the Constitution
Every citizen has the fundamental right to administrative action that is lawful, reasonable and procedurally fair.
Your information is protected
Personal information is processed under POPIA (Act 4 of 2013) and access is limited to the officers handling your case.
You can escalate to IPID
Statutory referrals under the IPID Act (Act 1 of 2011) happen automatically when the 72-hour clock is breached.

faq

Questions people ask first

Short answers to the things people want to know before they trust the system with a case.

What happens if a constable refuses to open my case?

Your protected submission and audio deposition are already recorded, so a refusal cannot make the report disappear. If the docket is not registered within 72 hours, the case is automatically frozen and escalated to IPID for independent review.

How does IPID monitor my docket?

Every status change, assignment and finding is written to an append-only audit trail. IPID reviewers see the same trail, and statutory referrals are generated automatically when the service standard is breached.

Can a docket or recording be deleted or altered?

No. Records are append-only and hash-sealed (SHA-256 chain of custody). A correction is logged as a new version under POPIA s24 while the original assertion is preserved untouched.

What proof do I get that I reported?

You receive a protected reference number, a timestamped receipt and an audio-backed deposition. You can track the case, add information or escalate from the citizen portal at any time.
